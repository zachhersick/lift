Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Lift is a workout, bodyweight and nutrition tracker. This policy explains what Lift collects, why, who handles it and how you delete it. Lift has no ads, doesn't track you across apps or websites, and never sells your data.

What Lift collects

  • Account: the verified email address from Apple, Google or GitHub when you sign in (with Sign in with Apple this can be a private relay address), and the name that service shares.
  • Profile: the username and display name you choose.
  • Training and health data you log: workouts, exercises and sets, splits, bodyweight, meals with calories, macros and micronutrients, goals, targets and settings.
  • Social: your friends, friend requests, people you block and reports you send.

Lift doesn't collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or device analytics.

Why

Only to run the app for you: to store and sync your logs between your devices, compute your progress, badges and Coach cards, show friends what you chose to share and, if you turn it on, write your weekly review.

Where it's kept

Your logs are stored on your device and, when you're signed in, on Lift's server so your devices stay in sync. The server runs on Vercel and stores data in a Neon Postgres database in the United States. Data is encrypted in transit.

What friends see

Only people you accept as friends see your profile: your display name, username, badges, streak, workout counts and pinned lift, plus, unless you turn them off in Profile, your best lifts, recent workouts and lifts as a multiple of bodyweight. Friends never see your email, bodyweight, meals or notes.

Services that process your data

  • Apple, Google or GitHub verify who you are when you sign in. Lift receives your email and name.
  • Vercel hosts Lift and Neon stores its database.
  • Anthropic (Claude) writes the weekly review, only for members with reviews turned on and only after you agree in the Coach. Lift sends a summary of your last six weeks (workouts, meals, weigh-ins and targets), never your email or name. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, this data isn't used to train its models.
  • OpenAI (ChatGPT) or Anthropic (Claude), only if you connect Lift to that assistant yourself and approve it. It can then log and change meals, read your day's nutrition, read a summary of your training, bodyweight and progress, and read, add and edit your splits and exercises through Lift's connector. It can't see or change your logged workouts. You can disconnect it at any time in the assistant's settings. What you share in those assistants is covered by OpenAI's or Anthropic's own privacy policy and your settings with them, not by the commercial terms for the weekly review above.

How long it's kept, and deleting it

Your data is kept until you delete it. In Lift, open Profile → Settings → Delete account. That permanently deletes your account and everything in it from Lift's server, removes it from the device you delete from, ends your friendships, revokes Sign in with Apple and disconnects ChatGPT. Copies in the database provider's backups expire within 30 days. You can also export a backup of your data at any time from Settings.

Children

Lift isn't directed to children under 13, and they shouldn't use it.

Your rights

You can see, export, correct and delete your data in the app. For anything else, including requests under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, email zachhersick31@gmail.com.
