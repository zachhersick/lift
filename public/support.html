Signing in and syncing

Sign in with Apple, Google or GitHub from Settings. Lift works offline; changes sync a couple of seconds after you're back online. Settings shows when it last synced and anything still waiting.

Friends

Everyone picks a username. Add friends by username in Profile; they see your request and accept it. Profile → What friends see controls what you share.

Reporting or blocking someone

Open their profile and tap Report or Block. You can also report or block someone from a friend request. Blocked people can't find or add you; unblock them in Profile. Reports are reviewed and abusive accounts are removed.

Backups

Settings → Export backup saves everything as a file, and Import backup brings it back.

Deleting your account

Profile → Settings → Delete account permanently deletes your account and all its data. Export a backup first if you want a copy.
